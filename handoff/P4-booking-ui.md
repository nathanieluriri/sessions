# P4. Booking flow, pricing and service pages, home page content, admin catalog UI

Read first: `.gauntlet/BRIEF.md`, `.gauntlet/ARCHITECTURE.md`, `.gauntlet/API-NOTES.md` (exact JSON the server returns, written from the engine builder's report: trust it over the prose), then the code. The server for all of this is done and tested (P2). The data is in `migrations/0005_catalog_sessions.sql`: read it, it is the catalog you are presenting.

## Goal

A visitor on a phone can pick what they are making and book it in the fewest steps that still make sense, for every kind of package: a fixed recording session, the Saturday All night session, a remote order (beat, mixing and mastering, song cover), and a premium EP or album request. The site's own pages (home, services, pricing, service pages, footer) present the new catalog, fixed session times and exclusive offers correctly and plainly. Staff can edit all of it from the dashboard.

## Bar

Match or beat Cal.com's public booking page for clarity of choosing a time slot on a phone (one decision per screen, availability obvious, no dead ends), and Stripe's pricing page for how plainly each package says what you get, what it costs and what happens next. Standing criterion: it must not read as a framework default; it uses the existing visual language (Reddit Sans and Mono, neon `#65f87c` on near black, tactile buttons, `.card`, `.opt`, `.chip`).

## Must do

1. **Step 1, what are you making.** Services grouped by category in category order, so Music and audio then Exclusive offers. Each card shows name and price label. Exclusive offers get a distinct premium treatment (badge, not a new design system). A service with no fixed bookable package (Artist development) shows "Request a quote" and goes to `/quote?service=`. Old service slugs redirect: `vocal-recording`, `voice-over`, `song-arrangement` to `recording`; `mixing`, `mastering`, `vocal-editing` to `mixing-mastering` (service detail route and any stored draft `serviceSlug`).
2. **Steps adapt to the package.** Compute the step list from the chosen package: sessions (`slot`, `day`): Service, Package (only if the service has more than one fixed package), Date and time, Details, Checkout; remote orders (`project`): Service, Package (if more than one), Details, Checkout. `Steps` renders from that list on desktop and mobile. Back buttons and redirect guards follow the same list (a stale draft must never strand someone on a step that does not apply).
3. **Time step by mode**, using `GET /availability/month` and `/day` with `package`: `slot`: month calendar plus session tiles for the chosen date showing the window ("10:00am to 12:00noon"), the session name, Open or Booked, and for the night kind a plain note that it is Saturdays only and ends Sunday 5:00am; selecting a tile sets `start`. Days with no windows are shown unavailable with a reason, not just greyed. `day`: calendar only, titled "Pick the day you want to start", with a short note that the studio confirms the full schedule and accommodation after approving. No extra-time control for non-flex modes. `flex` (staff-only packages still reachable by direct link) keeps today's behaviour. Windows crossing midnight read `11:00pm to 5:00am (Sunday)` everywhere.
4. **Details step by mode.** Remote orders: no "number of people", add an optional "Song title or titles" (stored in `project`), and a plain line about uploading stems or references in the portal right after payment. EP and album: label "People staying with us (including you)", capacity from the room, and the add-ons panel. Sessions: as today.
5. **Review and checkout.** Price table shows the regular price struck through with savings when `compare_at_kobo` is higher than the price, the unit ("per beat", "for 5 tracks"), extras with `quote` extras shown as "Price on request, the studio quotes it after you send the request" and never added to the total, turnaround ("Delivered within 7 days", "Delivered within 1 day") and revisions ("Up to 3 revisions"). Approval packages keep the request flow with the 24 hour promise and the deposit or full choice after approval. Remote orders never show a hold countdown or "slot held" text (the hold is only the checkout container). Deposit versus full only appears when the package has a deposit option.
6. **After booking.** Confirmation (`BookConfirmed`) and `BookRequested`: for remote orders, a clear primary action "Upload your files" linking to `/account/bookings/<id>#files` and a line saying finished work arrives in the portal (never by email); for sessions the ticket shows the session name and window (overnight aware); EP and album requests say what happens next. `BookVerify` receipt lines use unit and window correctly.
7. **Pricing page.** Order: Exclusive offers as a prominent band first or second (your call, justify it in the report), then Music and audio. Cards show price, unit, regular price and savings when present, turnaround, revisions, includes and the right button ("Book this package", "Request this package", "Request a quote"). Add a "Session times" section listing the four daily sessions and the All night session from `cat.sessions` (never hard-coded), and a short "How delivery works" strip (everything is delivered in your portal, files unlock when your balance is paid, 3 revisions on mixing and mastering) linking to FAQs. The "Rooms and extra time" section only appears when a bookable `flex` package exists. The badge text "Most booked" becomes "Featured". Policies grid unchanged except it now shows the new texts.
8. **Services pages, home page, footer.** Services page lists Exclusive offers. Service detail shows only the packages for that service (`service_slug`, falling back to category) with the same card. Home: `SHOWCASE` slugs, ribbon text and the Plans section stop depending on removed slugs (Plans picks `featured` packages, at most three, ordered by `sort`; never hard-code package slugs; fall back to the first three bookable packages); `PhoneDemo` and `demoData` use the real Recording session package and the real session windows. Footer and contact card hours: weekdays from the hours table plus a separate line for the All night session built from `cat.sessions` (for example "Saturday night · 11:00pm to 5:00am").
9. **Mobile.** At 390 px wide every booking screen has no horizontal scroll, the sticky action bar never hides content, tap targets are at least 44 px, and the fixed navigation pills no longer collide with the page heading (see the screenshot at `/tmp/claude-0/-home-user-sessions/e4158143-0612-583f-a6cc-25c7b8ce3d86/images/2.webp`: on the booking pages the nav floats over the "What are you making?" heading; fix this properly for the booking, pricing and service pages, for example with correct top spacing and a solid or fading backdrop behind the pills once scrolled, whichever fits the design best).
10. **Admin catalog UI** (`pages/admin/catalog.tsx`, `kit.tsx`): package form gets Service (dropdown of services in the category), How it is booked (a plain-language segmented control: Fixed session time, Whole day request, Remote order, Flexible start time), Session kind (day or night, only for fixed session time), Unit text, Regular price (optional, with hint "shows the discount"), Delivery days, Revisions, Number of tracks; show these columns in the package table (booking mode, delivery, revisions). Extras editor: service scope and "Price on request" (`pricing: quote`). A new "Session times" tab managing `session_slots` through the existing collection editor with a `time` field type (hours and minutes, with a "ends the next day" option so 23:00 to 05:00 is expressible), weekday select (Every day or Sunday to Saturday), kind select, name. All validation messages friendly.
11. **Tests.** Extract pure client logic (step list from a package, savings and percent, window label, service slug redirects) into `src/client/lib/` and cover it with unit tests under `tests/`. `npm run typecheck`, `npm test` and `npm run build` pass.
